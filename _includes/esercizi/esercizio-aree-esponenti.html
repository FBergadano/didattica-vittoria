Esercizio — Aree e volumi

i) Converti $1\ \text{µm}^3$ in megametri cubi (Mm³).

× 10 Mm³
$1\ µ\text{m}=10^{-6}\ \text{m}=10^{-12}\ \text{Mm}$, quindi $1\ µ\text{m}^3=(10^{-12})^3\ \text{Mm}^3=10^{-36}\ \text{Mm}^3$.

ii) Converti $67{,}12\ \text{nm}^2$ in metri quadri (m²).

× 10 m²
$1\ \text{nm}=10^{-9}\ \text{m}$, quindi $1\ \text{nm}^2=10^{-18}\ \text{m}^2$. Perciò $67{,}12\ \text{nm}^2=67{,}12\times10^{-18}\ \text{m}^2=6{,}712\times10^{-17}\ \text{m}^2$.

iii) Converti $5\,123\,456\ \text{m}^2$ in nanometri quadri (nm²).

× 10 nm²
$1\ \text{m}=10^{9}\ \text{nm}$, quindi $1\ \text{m}^2=10^{18}\ \text{nm}^2$. Perciò $5\,123\,456\ \text{m}^2=5{,}123456\times10^{6}\times10^{18}\ \text{nm}^2=5{,}123456\times10^{24}\ \text{nm}^2$.

iv) Converti $3{,}45\ \text{mm}^3$ in centimetri cubi (cm³).

× 10 cm³
$1\ \text{mm}=10^{-1}\ \text{cm}$, quindi $1\ \text{mm}^3=10^{-3}\ \text{cm}^3$. Perciò $3{,}45\ \text{mm}^3=3{,}45\times10^{-3}\ \text{cm}^3$.

v) Converti $0{,}0025\ \text{km}^2$ in metri quadri (m²).

× 10 m²
$1\ \text{km}=10^{3}\ \text{m}$, quindi $1\ \text{km}^2=10^{6}\ \text{m}^2$. Perciò $0{,}0025\ \text{km}^2=0{,}0025\times10^{6}\ \text{m}^2=2{,}5\times10^{3}\ \text{m}^2$.

vi) Converti $7{,}1\ \text{cm}^2$ in metri quadri (m²).

× 10 m²
$1\ \text{cm}=10^{-2}\ \text{m}$, quindi $1\ \text{cm}^2=10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$. Perciò $7{,}1\ \text{cm}^2=7{,}1\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$.

vii) Converti $12{,}6\ \text{Gm}^3$ in metri cubi (m³).

× 10 m³
$1\ \text{Gm}=10^{9}\ \text{m}$, quindi $1\ \text{Gm}^3=10^{27}\ \text{m}^3$. Perciò $12{,}6\ \text{Gm}^3=12{,}6\times10^{27}\ \text{m}^3=1{,}26\times10^{28}\ \text{m}^3$.
