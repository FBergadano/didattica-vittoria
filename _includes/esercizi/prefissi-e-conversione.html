Esercizio 7 — Conversione tra prefissi

i) Converti $5\ \text{km}$ in metri.

× 10 m
Soluzione: $5\ \text{km}=5\times10^3\ \text{m}$ — k ha esponente 3, base ha 0, diff 3.

ii) Converti $3\ \text{mm}$ in metri.

× 10 m
Soluzione: $3\ \text{mm}=3\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}$ — m ha esponente −3.

iii) Converti $7\ \text{Mm}$ in chilometri.

× 10 km
Soluzione: $7\ \text{Mm}=7\times10^3\ \text{km}$ — diff M→k: 6−3=3.

iv) Converti $500\ \text{µm}$ in millimetri.

× 10 mm
Soluzione: $500\ \text{µm}=0{,}5\ \text{mm}$ — diff μ→m: −6−(−3)=−3, quindi ÷10³.

v) Converti $1{,}5\ \text{km}$ in millimetri.

× 10 mm
Soluzione: $1{,}5\ \text{km}=1{,}5\times10^6\ \text{mm}$ — diff k→m: 3−(−3)=6.

vi) Converti $4{,}2\ \text{nm}$ in picometri.

× 10 pm
Soluzione: $4{,}2\ \text{nm}=4200\ \text{pm}$ — diff n→p: −9−(−12)=3.

vii) Converti $8\ \text{Mg}$ in grammi.

× 10 g
Soluzione: $8\ \text{Mg}=8\times10^6\ \text{g}$ — M ha esponente 6.

viii) Converti $0{,}3\ \text{mm}$ in micrometri.

× 10 μm
Soluzione: $0{,}3\ \text{mm}=300\ \text{µm}$ — diff m→μ: −3−(−6)=3.

ix) Converti $6{,}4\ \text{Gm}$ in Megametri.

× 10 Mm
Soluzione: $6{,}4\ \text{Gm}=6400\ \text{Mm}$ — diff G→M: 9−6=3.

x) Converti $25\ \text{cm}$ in metri.

× 10 m
Soluzione: $25\ \text{cm}=0{,}25\ \text{m}$ — diff c→base: −2−0=−2, quindi 25×10⁻²=0,25.
